The studio

Katha Entertainment Studios is a feature film and digital content company founded by Shibasish Sarkar.

Katha’s team brings extensive experience across Indian and international film distribution, production, and content monetisation. Its collective track record includes more than 400 films released and over US$1 billion in global box office, while its international distribution and licensing network encompasses key markets including the UK, USA, North America, Australia, and New Zealand.

Founder Shibasish Sarkar brings more than three decades of leadership experience across the media and entertainment industry. Prior to founding Katha Entertainment Studios, he served as Group CEO of Reliance Entertainment and has been involved in more than 500 films during his career across production, distribution, and studio leadership.

His experience includes major Hindi films such as Golmaal Again, Simmba, Super 30, Sooryavanshi, 83, Vikram Vedha and Singham Again, as well as streaming projects including Sacred Games, Sunflower, Jubilee, Indian Police Force and Chamkila.

With Bolo Bolo Bolo and Guru, Katha Entertainment Studios has commenced its first feature film productions. Further details will be announced in due course.
